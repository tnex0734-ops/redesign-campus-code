# 05 — Artwork and Asset Specification

## Purpose

The page requires a small but highly intentional art set. The supplied screenshots establish the art direction; they are not to be pasted into the website as full-page images.

## Asset 01 — Hero botanical specimen

Create one original vector illustration or layered image composition with:
- two or three overlapping leaves of different silhouettes;
- at least one full-colour leaf with visible veins;
- at least one translucent / pale leaf rendered as fine botanical linework;
- one large vein drawing using dark olive or muted brass linework;
- asymmetrical, organic leaf shapes;
- subtle tonal variation and paper transparency;
- no photorealistic stock leaf cutout with a hard white background.

The botanical illustration should feel like a specimen plate from an old natural history book, carefully redrawn with contemporary precision.

Suggested composition: a deep green leaf at the lower-left/centre; a translucent pale leaf overlapping it; a delicate gold/olive vein drawing extending upward. Preserve enough negative space for the hero text.

## Asset 02 — Hanging artefacts

Create five to seven small original SVG/CSS artefacts, each with:
- a distinct outer silhouette;
- a restrained colour field;
- one simple meaningful symbol;
- clean edges and a slight tactile/shadow detail;
- no text too small to read;
- a stable attachment point for the cord.

Suggested object set:
1. Challenge — small folded paper tag with a challenge mark.
2. Team — connected people/node motif.
3. Build — bracket/code or small project tile.
4. Calendar — compact square date page.
5. Ship — paper plane / launch mark.
6. Achievement — small rosette or leaf medal.
7. Community — connected-node ring.

Do not use generic fintech icons (wallet, card, transfer, bank) from reference 01. Adapt the tactile object treatment to CampusCode's actual product concepts.

## Asset 03 — Scientific grid

Build with CSS background gradients or a lightweight SVG:
- fine, low-contrast vertical/horizontal rules;
- wide enough spacing to resemble a plotted specimen sheet, not dense graph paper;
- a few sparse crosshair/registration marks;
- no dark grid lines behind long body copy.

The grid should become more visible in some editorial sections and nearly disappear behind text-heavy content.

## Asset 04 — Section reveal shapes

Create a limited set of reusable mask paths:
- leaf aperture;
- seed/irregular ellipse;
- paper sheet / folded corner;
- organic round crop;
- branching-vein line reveal.

Keep paths authored and fixed. Do not generate new random clip paths every render.

## Asset 05 — Small annotations

Use no more than a few of these per major composition:
- specimen number;
- short botanical note;
- coordinate-like number;
- tiny registration cross;
- dimension tick;
- fine leader line.

Annotations are decorative, should not pretend to be factual plant taxonomy, and should never become unreadable fake content. They must not resemble a repeated eyebrow label system.

## Production asset rules

- Prefer original SVG for leaf vein linework, strings, attachment dots and diagrams.
- Use CSS/HTML for interactive object cards, not flattened screenshots.
- Keep the supplied references in the repository under `assets/` for the agent to inspect.
- Do not use the whole moodboard as a background image.
- Do not crop out and use the Corona mark or text.
- Reuse the authentic CampusCode logo asset from the existing repo.
- Every image must have intrinsic dimensions / aspect ratio.
- Use responsive variants or `viewBox` cropping for mobile.
- Keep SVGs optimised and avoid excessive path counts.

## Illustration integrity

The botanical image should be visually detailed enough to be a hero signature. A single flat green leaf icon, stock photo leaf, generic monstera SVG or random leaf emoji is not an acceptable substitute.
